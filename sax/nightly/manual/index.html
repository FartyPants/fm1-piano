FM-1 TENOR SAX

FM-1 TENOR SAX manual

The FM-1 TENOR SAX is a tenor saxophone for the M-VAVE FM-1: Cranky Man's own recording, eight notes a minor third apart, each a recorded attack and a loop. It is in development: this manual grows with it.

Playing

  • One note at a time, like a sax. Keys held together slur (legato): the new note follows on without a new attack, from where the phrase has got to. A key let go before the next one is pressed starts a new, tongued note. Letting go of the top key of several held goes back, slurred, to the newest one still held.
  • A MIDI keyboard: into the FM-1's TRS MIDI IN jack, or over USB from a computer or a MIDI host. Notes on any channel, with velocity. CC 64 is the sustain pedal (down from 64): it holds the note past its key. The pitch wheel bends (BEND semitones each way). Channel pressure, and poly pressure on the note sounding, bend the note a little (AT DEPTH, AT SENS).
  • The FM-1's keys: F3 to G5. OCT− / OCT+ move them an octave (three down, two up); the header shows the shift. REC held is the sustain pedal. They play as hard as they are pressed (the Play page's TOUCH, on by default); with TOUCH off, at velocity 100 (FIXED). With AT KEYS on (the Expression page), pressing the key that sounds harder than it landed bends the note too: a light resting finger does nothing.
  • The release is the sound's own loop fading out, over REL.

Pages

  • HOME, the Sax page: the sound's name (TENOR SAX, a * once it is changed), the keys lit as they sound, the pitch's bend now (the pitch wheel, the pressure and the glide together, in cents) and the pressure. K1 TONE, K2 REL, K3 AT (the aftertouch's depth), K4 REVB (the reverb's level). HOME held a second puts the sound back as the preset has it.
  • ENV, Sound: TONE (a low-pass from 1 kHz to open), REL (the release, 20 ms to 2 s), GLIDE (a slurred note slides from the last one's pitch over this long; off: at once), VEL (the velocity curve: soft, normal, hard).
  • ARP, Expression: DEPTH (the bend at full pressure, -100 to 100 cents; below 0 it bends down, as a player's lip does), SENS (the pressure's sensitivity: higher reaches the full depth with less), KEYS (the FM-1's own keys send their pressure; off at first), BEND (the pitch wheel's range, 0 to 12 semitones).
  • LFO, EQ: BASS (120 Hz), MID (800 Hz), TREBL (5 kHz), each -12 to 12 dB; WIDTH (mono to a wide stereo; the sax stays in the middle when the two sides are added).
  • FX, Reverb: TYPE (Plate, Room, Studio, Hall, Church), DECAY, TONE, MIX. FX again, Delay: TIME, FDBK, TONE, MIX.
  • SEQ, Scale: MODE (OFF, LIGHT, MUTE, SNAP), KEY, SCALE. MUTE silences the notes outside the scale, SNAP moves them to it; LIGHT shows the scale on the keys.
  • GLO, Setup: CPU now and its peak, the voices sounding, MIDI the engine had no room for. K1 Volume, K2 Tune (A4 up or down, in cents).
  • GLO again, Play: K1 Touch, K2 Fixed (the velocity with Touch off), K3 Curve (how the press maps to velocity). With Touch on the page shows the last press.
  • SAVE: PRESET picks a user preset (USER 1 to 8), SAVE again stores the sound there and in the flash. Nothing is saved otherwise. The player's settings (Volume, Tune, VEL, BEND, the aftertouch, the scale, the touch) are global: a preset change keeps them.
